Who We Are

Rural Dignity Support Services is a Kenyan rural community organization working with underserved, marginalized, and vulnerable communities to improve health, dignity, inclusion, protection, wellbeing, and access to essential services.

Our approach combines community mobilization, health promotion, social support, inclusion, referral, education, and sustainable development partnerships.

Our Core Programmes

Comprehensive community-based initiatives tailored for maximum impact.

🏥 Primary Healthcare

Bringing essential health promotion, screening, education, referral, and mobile outreach closer to rural communities through health-worker engagement.

🤰 Mother & Child Health

Promoting antenatal/postnatal care, safe motherhood, newborn-care education, immunization awareness, nutrition, and early identification.

🧠 Mental Health & Psychosocial

Community-based mental health awareness, counselling, psychosocial support, trauma support, GBV-related support, and suicide prevention.

♿ Disability Inclusion

Promoting dignity, accessibility, participation, family support, and the empowerment of persons with disabilities while reducing stigma.

🧒 Children & Child Protection

Supporting vulnerable children and street-connected children through protection, healthcare, psychosocial support, education, and reintegration.

🚫 Substance-Use Recovery

Prevention and awareness, withdrawal support, recovery support, family counselling, rehabilitation referrals, and community reintegration.

🩺 NCD Prevention & Outreach

Hypertension and diabetes awareness/screening, cancer awareness, healthy lifestyle promotion, early detection, and specialist referrals.

🤝 Marginalized Communities

Social inclusion, community protection, support for vulnerable families, access to essential services, and community empowerment.

🌱 Youth & Community Development

Youth participation, life skills, community leadership, education, local mobilization, and grassroots development initiatives.
